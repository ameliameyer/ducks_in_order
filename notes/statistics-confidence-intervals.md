# Statistics: Confidence Intervals

**Learning status:** Starter note — not assessed.

A 95% confidence interval is produced by a procedure that covers the fixed population parameter in 95% of repeated samples under its assumptions. It is not a 95% probability statement about the parameter after observing a particular interval.

## Exercise

A sample estimates a conversion rate of 12% with a 95% confidence interval of 10%–14%. Explain what the interval means, and why it does not establish causality.
