How to spend each session

Study Guides: concept → example → breakdown → practice. Practice Lab: attempt independently first, then consult references, then use AI to review and debug. Projects: apply skills to realistic business problems. Assessments and Mastery: review mistakes and record evidence of progress.

Job applications

Apply now, not after finishing the curriculum. Aim for 5–10 quality applications per week when suitable openings exist; use additional time only when worthwhile. Suggested mix: 60% strong-fit analyst roles, 25% growth roles (product analytics or analytics engineering), 15% data scientist stretch roles. Apply promptly to strong matches and use recurring job requirements to prioritize learning.

Busy-week fallback (3 hours)

60 minutes independent coding, 60 minutes learning or applied project work, 30 minutes review, 30 minutes applications. Resume the normal plan when possible.

Six-month career focus

Suggested focus areas beginning December 2026. Advance based on demonstrated understanding, not the calendar. Continue reviewing earlier topics throughout.

December · Technical foundations

Advanced SQL, CTEs, windows, Python, pandas, Git and debugging. Evidence: solve unfamiliar SQL and Python problems.

January · Data architecture

Normalization, dimensional modeling, fact and dimension tables, ETL/ELT, Snowflake and dbt. Evidence: build and test a small warehouse model.

February · Applied statistics

Probability, inference, regression, experimentation and causal reasoning. Evidence: analyze an experiment and communicate uncertainty.

March · Machine learning

Regression, classification, clustering, features, validation, leakage and metrics. Evidence: compare models against a baseline.

April · Production data science

Reproducibility, tests, pipelines, APIs, deployment and monitoring. Evidence: package a reproducible model workflow.

May · AI and integration

LLMs, embeddings, retrieval, evaluation, responsible AI and a capstone. Evidence: validate an AI-assisted solution and explain its limitations.

Domain knowledge across all phases

Rotate business cases across SaaS retention, product funnels, finance, operations, marketing, healthcare and e-commerce. Learn the metrics, business context, data limitations and decisions behind each analysis.

Monthly review

Check what you can explain, solve independently and apply to a new problem. Review portfolio evidence and job requirements; adjust the next month's focus accordingly. These are learning targets, not guarantees of job readiness.
